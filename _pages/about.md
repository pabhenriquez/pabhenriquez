I am an Associate Professor at the [Facultad de Administración y Economía](http://economiayempresa.udp.cl/), Universidad Diego Portales, Santiago, Chile. I am also co-Director of the Master in Business Analytics and Data Science, and Director of the Master in Digital Business and Data Analytics (Full time) and the Master in Digital Business.

I received my B.Sc. (2012), P.E. and M.Sc. (2015) degrees in Physics from Universidad de Concepción, Chile, and my Ph.D. (2019) in Complex Systems Engineering from Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, Chile.

My research interests include **machine learning**, **data science**, **business intelligence**, **data mining**, **business analytics**, **deep learning** and **neural networks with random weights**.

I serve as Academic Editor of *PLOS ONE* and *PLOS Complex Systems*, and as Associate Editor (Artificial Intelligence section) of *Sage Open*. I am also co-founder of Whitebox S.A., a data analytics consulting firm.
